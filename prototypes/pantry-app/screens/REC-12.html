Kimchi fried rice

Prep 10 min · Cook 10 min · Recipe serves 2, scaled to 8
At 8 servings you're short of 3 things, and the rice needs more than your pack holds.Back to 2 servings

Ingredients

Serves8
1.2 kg brown rice, cooked and cooledBrown rice · have 1 bag (1 kg), openToo little
Needs 1.2 kg. You have 1 bag, which holds 1 kg at most, and it's open.
4 jars kimchi, choppedKimchi · have 1 jarShort 3
8 eggsEggs · have 4Short 4
8 spring onions, slicedSpring onions · none in stockShort
4 tbsp soy sauceSoy sauce · have 1 bottle, open
2 tbsp sesame oilSesame oil · have 1 bottle, open
4 cloves garlicGarlic · have 1 bulb

Method

  1. 1Fry the garlic and spring onion whites in the sesame oil for a minute.
  2. 2Add the kimchi and fry until it starts to catch.
  3. 3Add the rice and soy sauce. Toss over a high heat until hot through.
  4. 4Push to one side, fry the eggs, then serve on top with the spring onion greens.
