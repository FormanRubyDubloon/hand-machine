Sardine linguine

Prep 5 minCook 15 minServes 2Last cooked NeverSource Pasted text, added by Claude 10 Nov
You have 6 of 8. Linguine and parsley are on your shopping list.See the list

Method

  1. 1Boil the linguine in salted water until just tender. Keep a cup of the cooking water.
  2. 2Warm the olive oil in a wide pan. Fry the breadcrumbs until golden, then tip them out.
  3. 3Add the garlic and chilli flakes to the pan and cook gently for a minute.
  4. 4Add the sardines with their oil and break them up. Stir in the lemon zest and juice.
  5. 5Toss the pasta through with a splash of the cooking water. Top with the parsley and breadcrumbs.

My notes

Good with extra lemon. Toast the breadcrumbs for longer than seems right.
