Sardine linguine

Prep 5 min · Cook 15 min · Never cooked · Added by Claude, 10 Nov
You have 6 of 8. Linguine and parsley are on your shopping list.See the list

Ingredients · 8

Serves2
200 g linguineLinguine · none in stockShort
2 × 120 g tins sardines in olive oilSardines · have 7 tins
2 cloves garlic, slicedGarlic · have 1 bulb
1 lemon, zest and juiceLemons · have 2
½ tsp chilli flakesChilli flakes · have 1 jar, open
3 tbsp olive oilOlive oil · have 3 bottles, 1 open
Small bunch parsley, choppedParsley · none in stockShort
2 tbsp breadcrumbsBreadcrumbs · have 1 bag, open

Method

  1. 1Boil the linguine in salted water until just tender. Keep a cup of the cooking water.
  2. 2Warm the olive oil in a wide pan. Fry the breadcrumbs until golden, then tip them out.
  3. 3Add the garlic and chilli flakes to the pan and cook gently for a minute.
  4. 4Add the sardines with their oil and break them up. Stir in the lemon zest and juice.
  5. 5Toss the pasta through with a splash of the cooking water. Top with the parsley and breadcrumbs.
My notesGood with extra lemon. Toast the breadcrumbs for longer than seems right.
